Fernway is a task tracker for teams. A workspace holds your team's projects, and every project holds tasks. Sign up at fernway.example with your work email, choose a workspace name, and you land in an empty workspace on the Free plan.

To invite teammates, open Settings, then Members, and choose Invite. Enter one email address per line. Invitations expire after 7 days; you can resend one from the same page. On the Free plan a workspace can have up to 5 members.

Create your first project from the sidebar with New project. Pick a template (Product launch, Bug triage, Editorial calendar) or start blank. Templates add sample columns and tasks you can delete at any time.

Install the desktop app for macOS or Windows, or the mobile apps for iOS and Android, from the Downloads page. All apps sign in to the same workspace and stay in sync.
