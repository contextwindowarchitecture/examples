title: Deleting tasks, projects and workspaces
version: 3
updated: 2026-04-22T09:30:00Z
---
Deleted tasks and projects go to the trash, where they stay for 30 days. Restore them from Settings, then Trash. After 30 days they are deleted permanently and cannot be recovered by support.

Only a workspace Owner can delete a workspace, from Settings, then Data, then Delete workspace. The workspace is locked for a 14-day grace period, during which the Owner can cancel the deletion. After that, every project, task and attachment is deleted.

Export your workspace before deleting it if you might need the data later. Deleting a workspace also cancels its plan; an annual plan deleted within its 30-day refund window can still be refunded.
