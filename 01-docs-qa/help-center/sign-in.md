title: Trouble signing in
version: 6
updated: 2026-09-04T14:00:00Z
---
If you forgot your password, choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. Fernway emails a reset link that expires after 30 minutes. If no email arrives, check spam, then ask a workspace Admin to confirm the address on your account.

You can also sign in without a password with a magic link: enter your email and choose Email me a link. The link works once and expires after 15 minutes.

If you lost the device with your authenticator app, sign in with one of the recovery codes you saved when you set up two-factor authentication. Each code works once. Without a recovery code, a workspace Owner can reset your two-factor authentication from Settings, then Members.

Members of a workspace that requires SSO cannot sign in with a password. Use Sign in with SSO and enter your work email; if your identity provider is down, contact a workspace Owner.
