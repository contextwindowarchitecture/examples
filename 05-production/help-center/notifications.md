Fernway notifies you when you are assigned a task, mentioned in a comment, or when a task you follow changes status. Notifications arrive in the inbox at the top of the sidebar, and by email and push according to your settings.

Change what you receive under Profile, then Notifications. Each event type can go to the inbox, email, push, or nowhere. The daily digest sends one email at 08:00 in your time zone instead of one email per event.

Mute a busy project from its menu with Mute notifications. You still receive notifications for tasks assigned to you and for direct mentions. Quiet hours pause push notifications on mobile during the hours you choose.
