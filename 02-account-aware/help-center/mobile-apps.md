The Fernway apps for iOS and Android show your inbox, your assigned tasks and every project you can see. Download them from the App Store or Google Play and sign in with the same account as on the web.

The apps work offline. Tasks and comments you create without a connection are saved on the device and sync when you are back online. If someone else changed the same task meanwhile, the latest change to each field wins.

Push notifications follow your notification settings and quiet hours. To stop them on one device only, turn off notifications for Fernway in the device's own settings.
