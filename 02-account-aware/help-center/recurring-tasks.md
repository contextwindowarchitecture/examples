Recurring tasks repeat on a schedule: daily, on chosen weekdays, monthly on a date, or every N days. Set a schedule from the task's due date picker with Repeat. Recurring tasks are available on the Team and Business plans.

Fernway creates the next instance when you complete the current one, not on a fixed clock. The next due date is calculated from the schedule, so completing a weekly task late does not shift the following week's date.

To stop a recurring task, open the latest instance and choose Repeat, then Never. Earlier completed instances stay in the project's history.
