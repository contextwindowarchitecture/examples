Check status.fernway.example for the current state of the web app, the mobile sync service, the API and integrations. Subscribe there to get incident updates by email or RSS.

Business workspaces have a 99.9% monthly uptime commitment. If uptime falls below it, the workspace Owner can claim a service credit of 10% of that month's fee, or 25% below 99.0%, by contacting support within 30 days of the end of the month.

Scheduled maintenance is announced on the status page at least 72 hours in advance and does not count against the uptime commitment.
