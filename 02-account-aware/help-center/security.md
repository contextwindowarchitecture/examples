Fernway encrypts data in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Attachments are stored in the same region as the rest of the workspace's data.

Fernway holds a SOC 2 Type II report, renewed every year. Business customers can request the latest report under Settings, then Security, after accepting a non-disclosure agreement.

Every member can turn on two-factor authentication under Profile, then Security, with an authenticator app or a security key. Owners and Admins can require two-factor authentication for the whole workspace; members without it are asked to set it up at their next sign-in.

Business workspaces choose where their data is stored, in the EU or the US, when the workspace is created. Moving an existing workspace to another region is done by support and takes up to 5 business days.
