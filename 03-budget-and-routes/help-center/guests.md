Guests are people outside your company, such as clients or contractors, whom you invite to specific projects. Guests are available on the Team and Business plans and do not use a paid seat.

A workspace can have up to 5 guests for every paid member. Invite a guest from a project's Share menu by entering their email; they see that project only.

Guests can comment on tasks, complete tasks assigned to them, and upload attachments. They cannot see the workspace member list, other projects, or the workspace's integrations.
