The Slack integration posts project updates to a Slack channel and turns Slack messages into tasks. It is available on the Team and Business plans. An Admin connects it under Settings, then Integrations, then Slack.

To send a project's updates to a channel, open the project menu, choose Integrations, then Slack, and pick the channel. You choose which events are posted: new tasks, completed tasks, comments, or all of them.

To create a task from a Slack message, use the message's More actions menu and choose Create Fernway task. The task links back to the message, and the message's text becomes the task description.
